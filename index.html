What gets redacted?

Common ASCII emails, IPv4, secret assignments, Bearer/Basic values, selected tokens, URL credentials, and complete PEM private keys. IPv6, cookies, paths, names, and custom formats are not covered automatically; add exact values above. False positives are possible.

Where does my text go?

Redaction happens in this browser. The tool makes no log uploads and uses no analytics or persistent text storage. Clear all removes text and custom values from the interface. Your clipboard and downloaded files remain under your control.
